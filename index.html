About ImpactBridge

The Trusted Gateway Between Swiss Capital and Vietnamese Communities

Bridging geographic, cultural, and governance distances so Swiss companies can create genuine, measurable, and risk-free social impact.

Why Switzerland & Vietnam?

Switzerland is globally recognized for high governance standards, rigorous sustainability mandates, and committed corporate citizenship. Meanwhile, Vietnam is experiencing vibrant economic growth alongside critical social and environmental challenges — from vulnerable rural education to climate threats in the Mekong Delta.

However, cross-border corporate social responsibility (CSR) faces huge hurdles: lack of local visibility, language barriers, complex local regulations, and concerns over fund transparency.

ImpactBridge is that trusted bridge. With teams in Zurich and Vietnam, we provide the complete on-the-ground infrastructure: vetting grassroot partners, managing compliance, conducting unscheduled audits, and delivering audit-ready ESG impact reports in English, German, and French.

Swiss Precision & Accountability

Contracts, milestone approvals, and reporting designed for Swiss compliance standards.

Native On-Site Execution in Vietnam

Local field officers physically inspect progress, conduct interviews, and track budget utilization.
